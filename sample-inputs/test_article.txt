Nexura Dynamics Closes $42M Series B for Clean Energy AI Orchestration
========================================================================

Zurich, Switzerland — August 28, 2026

Nexura Dynamics, an innovator in autonomous clean-energy grid management, today announced the successful closing of its $42 million Series B financing round led by Horizon Climate Ventures.

The funding will accelerate global commercial deployment of HeliosGrid-4, an edge-computed orchestration unit that reduces industrial facility electricity wastage by up to 37%.

1. EXPANSION PLANS
The company plans to expand its research center in Zurich and establish regional field support hubs across 5 international territories before Q4 2026.

2. CUSTOMER ROI AND PAYBACK
Customer data across 18 active commercial pilot deployments demonstrated an average return on investment timeline of under 2.1 years, making clean microgrid adoption financially advantageous without relying on regional subsidy programs.

# Autonomous Microgrid Orchestration with HeliosGrid-4

## Executive Summary
HeliosGrid-4 is a next-generation decentralized energy management platform designed to balance renewable generation, commercial battery storage, and dynamic grid loads in real time. 

## Key Technical Specifications
- Peak Power Capacity: 250 kW per module
- Round-Trip Energy Efficiency: 94.6%
- Sub-cycle Switching Latency: < 12 milliseconds
- Compatible Protocols: Modbus TCP, IEEE 2030.5, OpenADR 2.0b

## Commercial Deployment Results
In extensive industrial trials spanning 18 commercial manufacturing facilities, HeliosGrid-4 delivered:
1. An average facility energy cost reduction of 37.4%.
2. Peak demand charge mitigation saving over $84,000 annually per site.
3. Zero unplanned outages over a 14-month continuous operating window.

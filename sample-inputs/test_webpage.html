Introducing LuminaFlow: The Context-Aware Team Workspace

LuminaFlow is an AI-powered assistant designed to eliminate fragmented communication and context-switching for modern distributed teams.

Core Capabilities

  • Automated Standup Synthesis: Summarizes daily project blockers across GitHub, Jira, and Slack.
  • Smart Meeting Action Items: Transcribes calls and automatically populates assignee dashboards.
  • Predictive Deadline Tracking: Flags scheduling bottlenecks before milestones slip.

Pricing and Availability

LuminaFlow is free for teams up to 10 members. Enterprise tiers with SOC-2 Type II certification and custom LLM routing start at $12 per seat per month.

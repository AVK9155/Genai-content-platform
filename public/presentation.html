Problem Statement & Abstract

The Content Fragmentation Dilemma

Modern organizations produce high volumes of technical, financial, and product documents, but re-purposing them across communication channels creates significant operational bottlenecks.

⏳
Repetitive Manual Labor
Marketing and communications teams spend 65%+ of their time manually rewriting whitepapers into blogs, social threads, and emails.
⚠️
Hallucinations & Context Drift
Standard LLM chats frequently invent statistics, lose core technical nuance, or distort critical numbers across channels.
📉
Format Incompatibility
Legacy systems fail to ingest tabular data (CSV/TSV), subtitle timestamps (SRT/VTT), or rich PDF hierarchy without loss of structure.
Core Innovation

The Autonomous Transformation Engine

GENAI bridges raw multi-modal ingestion with contextual generation through a deterministic 4-stage pipeline.

🧬
AST & Document Structure Preservation
Rather than treating uploaded documents as a flat string of words, GENAI computes font-size ratios in PDFs, parses semantic HTML in Word documents, converts CSV/TSV into aligned Markdown tables, and cleans subtitle timestamps to retain document context.
🔍
Real-Time Two-Pass Grounding Verifier
Every generated format passes through an automated second-pass fact-checking prompt that cross-verifies names, figures, and technical claims against the original document before presenting a green ✓ Grounded or amber ⚠ Review badge.
System Pipeline

End-to-End Execution Workflow

How a single document flows from raw ingestion to verified, exported multi-channel deliverables.

STAGE 01
Ingest & Normalize
Client-side parsing of PDF, Word, CSV, JSON, RTF, SRT or text with AST hierarchy reconstruction.
STAGE 02
Brand Voice Injection
Database fetches user's active Brand Voice Profile (tone guidelines, vocabulary constraints, reading level).
STAGE 03
Parallel LLM Synthesis
Backend orchestrates JSON-structured prompt generating all selected output formats in a single pass.
STAGE 04
Grounding & Export
Outputs verified for hallucinations; rendered in side-by-side editor with one-click export (.docx, .pdf, .txt, .pptx).
